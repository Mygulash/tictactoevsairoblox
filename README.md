This is an TIC TAC TOE vs AI project in Roblox 

Every scripts is done in Luau.

When you join and you touch the join part next to the Gomoku board you get to chose from 2 gamemodes:
1) ME vs AI - where you can play against actual AI (it is not that smart because roblox has many limitations on calculating)
2) ME vs Player - you can actually play against another player 

To win the game you have to connect 5 stones in row.
